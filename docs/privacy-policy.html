Nuvexa — Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 24, 2026

Overview

Nuvexa is an offline-first utility app — calculators, converters, generators, and everyday tools that run entirely on your device. This policy explains what Nuvexa does and does not do with your data.

Data we collect

Nuvexa does not require or create a user account. It does not upload your photos, documents, text, or clipboard content anywhere, and it does not use any cloud service to run its tools.

Information such as your favorites, history entries, and app settings (theme, language, preferences) is stored in the app's private local storage on your device. Nuvexa itself never transmits it anywhere — see "Android backup" below for the one way Android's own OS-level backup can still move some of it off the device. It is deleted if you clear the app's data or uninstall it.

Permissions we ask for, and why

  • Camera — used by the QR/barcode scanner to read a code pointed at the camera, and separately by the "scan text" (OCR) tools to capture a photo that Nuvexa then reads text out of. In both cases the camera feed and any captured photo are processed only on your device and are never uploaded anywhere.
  • Internet / network state — used only to serve optional advertising through Google AdMob (see below). Every tool in Nuvexa works fully offline; this permission is not required for the app's core functionality.
  • Vibrate — used for optional haptic feedback on button presses, controlled by a setting in the app.

Nuvexa requests each permission only when the relevant tool is actually opened, not at launch.

Text recognition (OCR)

The tools that "extract text" from a photo, the camera, or a PDF use Google ML Kit's on-device text recognizer, running with a model bundled inside the app. The image is processed locally and is never sent to a server or to Google for this — recognition works the same with your network connection fully off.

Currency rates

The Currency tool uses fixed reference rates stored in the app, not a live market feed — Nuvexa has no server and does not fetch exchange rates over the network. The app makes this clear in the tool itself; do not rely on it for rates that must reflect the current market.

Advertising

Nuvexa may show ads served by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and process data (such as an advertising identifier) according to Google's own privacy and consent practices, independent of Nuvexa. You can review Google's policies at policies.google.com/privacy. Advertising is the only network activity Nuvexa performs — no tool's functionality depends on it, and every tool works the same with the network fully off.

Data retention and your choices

You are always in control of what Nuvexa stores: clear your history or favorites from within the app's Settings at any time, or remove all app data by uninstalling Nuvexa or clearing its storage from your device's system settings.

Android backup

Nuvexa itself has no server and never uploads your favorites, history, or settings anywhere — but Android's own backup system, which Nuvexa does not control, can. Your app settings (theme, language, and similar preferences) may be included in Android's standard device backup/restore and device-to-device transfer features, the same as most apps. Favorites and history entries are deliberately excluded from Google's cloud backup, since they can reflect what you calculated, converted, or encrypted, but they are included when moving data directly to a new device you set up yourself. You can turn off Android's backup for Nuvexa (or any app) from your device's system settings at any time.

Children's privacy

Nuvexa is a general-audience utility app and is not directed at children under 13. It does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this same address with a new "last updated" date above.

Contact
