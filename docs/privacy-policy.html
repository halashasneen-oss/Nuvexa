Nuvexa — Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 15, 2026

Overview

Nuvexa is an offline-first utility app — calculators, converters, generators, and everyday tools that run entirely on your device. This policy explains what Nuvexa does and does not do with your data.

Data we collect

Nuvexa does not require or create a user account. It does not upload your photos, documents, text, or clipboard content anywhere, and it does not use any cloud service to run its tools.

Information such as your favorites, history entries, and app settings (theme, language, preferences) is stored only in the app's private local storage on your device. It is never transmitted to Nuvexa or to any third party, and it is deleted if you clear the app's data or uninstall it.

Permissions we ask for, and why

  • Camera — used only when you open the QR/barcode scanner tool, to read a code pointed at the camera. No photo or video is stored or sent anywhere.
  • Internet / network state — used only to serve optional advertising through Google AdMob (see below). Every tool in Nuvexa works fully offline; this permission is not required for the app's core functionality.
  • Vibrate — used for optional haptic feedback on button presses, controlled by a setting in the app.

Nuvexa requests each permission only when the relevant tool is actually opened, not at launch.

Advertising

Nuvexa may show ads served by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and process data (such as an advertising identifier) according to Google's own privacy and consent practices, independent of Nuvexa. You can review Google's policies at policies.google.com/privacy. Advertising is the only network activity Nuvexa performs — no tool's functionality depends on it, and every tool works the same with the network fully off.

Data retention and your choices

Because everything Nuvexa stores lives only on your device, you are always in control of it: clear your history or favorites from within the app's Settings at any time, or remove all app data by uninstalling Nuvexa or clearing its storage from your device's system settings.

Children's privacy

Nuvexa is a general-audience utility app and is not directed at children under 13. It does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this same address with a new "last updated" date above.

Contact

Questions about this policy or Nuvexa's data practices can be sent to support@nuvexa.app.
